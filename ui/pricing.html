Questions

Before you decide

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. No card, no trial that turns into a charge. You get the full check and every fix, as often as you want to run it yourself.

Why isn't "you've been leaked" behind a paywall?

Because you deserve to know. We'd rather you trust us than feel tricked. Pro is for convenience: we keep watching so you don't have to.

Can I cancel Pro?

Any time, from your Account page, in two clicks. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel in the first 7 days, you get a full refund.

How do I pay?

UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking. Payments are handled by a secure payment provider; we never see your card number.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

You go back to Free. Saved history is deleted after 30 days unless you download it first.

Do you offer family plans?

Pro covers up to 5 accounts, so you can add family members' accounts with their permission.
